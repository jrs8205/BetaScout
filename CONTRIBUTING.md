# Contributing to BetaScout

Thanks for your interest in improving BetaScout!

## Translation corrections — the most wanted contribution

BetaScout ships community-proofread translations. If a wording in your
language sounds off, [open an issue](https://github.com/jrs8205/BetaScout/issues)
with the language, where the text appears, and your suggested wording.
Native-speaker corrections have already improved the app and are always
welcome.

## Bug reports

Open an issue and include:

- Device and Android version
- BetaScout version (shown at the bottom of the settings page)
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead

## Code changes

BetaScout is maintained by a single author. Before writing code, please open
an issue to discuss the change first — it keeps anyone from spending effort on
work that may not fit the project's direction.

Two hard lines for any change:

- **Privacy:** no ads, no analytics, no telemetry, no new data leaving the
  device. The optional catalog sharing (bare package names only) is the only
  exception, and it stays opt-in.
- **Honesty:** the app never joins a beta on the user's behalf and never
  overstates what it can detect.
